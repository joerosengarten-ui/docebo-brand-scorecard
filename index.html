What is the Brand Score?

The quarterly Brand Score measures the efficacy of Docebo's brand in the market based on five key inputs. It allows us to track brand health at a glance every quarter.

1For each metric, we compare actual performance against the target goal to get a performance percentage.
2Each metric is assigned a weight based on strategic importance. We multiply the performance percentage by the weight to get the score for that category.
3Over-performance is capped at 120%. Underperformance is floored at 0% — no metric can contribute a negative score.
4We sum the scores from all five categories to reach a final Brand Score between 0 and 100.

Branded Search TrafficWebsite traffic from direct and brand-led channels
